A good walk is not a route between two points. It is a way of letting the day come back into focus: the particular green of a traffic light, the bakery that opens before the city, the same dog waiting at the same corner.

I am trying to keep a record of these details without turning them into a project. The notes are the point. The archive is just what happens when you return.
