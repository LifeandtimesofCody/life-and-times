The web is at its best when it gives a person somewhere to put the things that do not fit anywhere else. Not a feed, not a pitch, not a performance: a shelf for unfinished thoughts and a map of where you have been.

This site is deliberately modest. It is meant to reward a little time, and to make it easy to find something again.
