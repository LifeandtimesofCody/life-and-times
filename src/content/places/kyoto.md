**Eat:** breakfast at Nishiki Market before the crowds arrive, then a bowl of soba somewhere you cannot pronounce.

**Skip:** trying to see every famous temple in one day. The quiet side street between them is usually the better memory.

**Do differently:** book the small places early, and spend an afternoon in one neighborhood instead of crossing the city.
