**Eat:** grilled sardines at a neighborhood tasca, still warm from the charcoal. Find a small counter and order what the table next to you is having.

**Skip:** the first restaurant with a laminated menu and a view of the tram line.

**Do differently:** leave one morning unplanned. Lisbon is better when the hills decide the itinerary.
